## 2 Purpose and summary

This plan is used by every control family. It holds every weakness that needs a fix, in one register (section 4), with closed items in section 5 and the road to authorisation in section 6. There are 131 items: 13 rated High, 55 Medium and 63 Low. Of these, 6 are fixed and awaiting a repeat test on the VMs, 2 are closed, and 123 remain open, of which 10 are rated High.

The self-assessments of all 19 families are complete. They record these items and make no fixes, which wait for the development phase. An item is worked on once its risk decision in \<Risk List\> is to remediate. An item whose risk has been accepted stays listed until the acceptance is reviewed.

In the Source column, a letter and number is a check in the evidence store. "Code review" means found by the assessor's review of the application and not tested. The finding is stated in full here, so no reader needs the code. "Assessment" and "Review" mean found while assessing or reviewing a family. Section 6 gives the recommended order of decisions and work, with the facts still to be supplied.
