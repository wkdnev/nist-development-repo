# \<System Name\> Record Templates

## 1 Purpose

An assessor looks for records that show a control operating, as well as a statement that it should. These templates make those records consistent. They are written for any family: each has a family field, and Table 1 shows which controls use it. Completed records are evidence, are kept in the restricted SharePoint area, and are not stored in this document.

Record numbers take the form \<template>-\<family>-\<number>, such as TPL-1-AC-001.

**Table 1: Templates and where they are used**

| --- | --- | --- |

| TPL-2 Access request | Records a request, its approval by the line manager and its authorisation by the group's owner before access changes. The ServiceNow access approval workflow is where requests are made, and the closed ServiceNow request is this record and must hold these fields | AC-2c, AC-2e, AC-6(5); PRO-AC-1, PRO-AC-2, PRO-AC-5, PRO-AC-6; IA-4, CM-5, PS-3; PRO-PS-1 |
