## 2 Purpose

This is the one place where approvals and decisions are made and recorded. Each row states what needs deciding, the recommendation and who decides, with space for the decision, name and date. Nothing is decided until a named person completes a row. The recommendations are proposals.

After a decision, the document it affects is updated to match: \<Access Control Management\> for values, \<Risk List\> for risk treatments and \<Tailoring Register\> for tailoring.

Decisions are numbered D-XX-nn, parameter approvals D-XX-Pnn and non-compliance entries NC-XX-nn, where XX is the family code. The AC entries keep the numbers NC-01 to NC-39, so that citations stay valid.

## 24 Proof of approval

Every decision in this register names the person who made it. Where an outcome says "as confirmed by the system owner", the system owner reported another person's decision, and that person has not yet signed for it. This section closes that gap.

The Evidence column of each table names the sign-off sheet that proves the decision. A sheet is form TPL-24 in \<Record Templates\>. It lists the decisions by number, and the approver signs it, or confirms it by email or in a minuted meeting. One sheet covers every decision a person has made up to its date, and a new sheet is started for later decisions. "None yet" marks a decision that is still pending. A row that covers several entries counts once for each entry.

Until a sheet is filed, the decisions it covers rest on the system owner's record alone. The system owner files each sheet in the Restricted store and enters its reference and date below. Nobody signs for anyone else.
