# \<System Name\> User Security Guide

## 1 Purpose

This guide tells you how to use \<System Name\>'s security and privacy features, how to work securely, and what you are responsible for (SA-5b). The rules you sign are in \<Security Operating Procedures\>, Part A; this guide explains how to follow them on screen.

## 2 Signing in and your password

1. Open \<System Name\> in your browser. You are sent to \<Organisation\>'s sign-in page, where you sign in with your usual \<Organisation\> account and your second factor.
2. \<System Name\> has no password of its own. Never type your \<Organisation\> password into a page you were not sent to by \<Organisation\>'s sign-in.
3. Follow \<Organisation\>'s rules for your password. If you forget it, or your account locks, ask \<Organisation\>'s IT service desk.
4. If you think anyone else knows your password, or you lose your second factor, tell the service desk and the security officer at once.

## 3 Using \<System Name\> securely

**Table 1: Security features you'll see**

| Feature | What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project access | You see only the projects your role allows | If you see a project you shouldn't, report it at once |
| Screen lock | \<System Name\> has no page lock of its own. Your \<Organisation\> device locks its screen after a set time without use | Lock your device whenever you leave it |
| Sign-in | Your sign-in lasts as long as \<Organisation\>'s PingFederate allows. \<System Name\> does not sign you out itself | Save your work; close the browser when you finish |
| Exports | You can export documents your role allows | Keep exports on \<Organisation\>'s devices; delete them within 30 days (SyOps A28) |
| Printing | Documents can be printed | Use secure-release printers only; never print at home (SyOps A33, A38) |

## 4 Personal data

1. Look at personal data only when your task needs it, and use it only for HR administration and managing project documents (SyOps A23).
2. Copy, export or print only the minimum (A24), and never share it with anyone who doesn't need it (A25).
3. If personal data is wrong or in the wrong project, tell the project access approver (A26).
4. Pass any request from a person about their data, or any complaint about how it's handled, to the legal adviser the same working day (A27).
5. \<Organisation\>'s employee privacy notice explains how your own data is used; \[link to the employee privacy notice\].

## 5 Your responsibilities

1. Read and sign the rules in \<Security Operating Procedures\>, Part A, before you get access, and again after any material change.
2. Complete security and privacy training before access and every 12 months (A54).
3. Report any loss, theft, misdirected message, unauthorised access or suspicious message about \<System Name\> to the security officer at once, and within 1 hour (A29).
